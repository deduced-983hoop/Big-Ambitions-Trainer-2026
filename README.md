## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a role-playing business simulation game developed by Hovgaard Games. It launched in Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Set in an open-world version of New York City, the game combines life simulation, business management, logistics, property management, and entrepreneurship.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer enhances the core business and life-simulation systems of Big Ambitions. It provides optional control over money, personal needs, time, inventory, business operations, and vehicle-related resources while preserving the game's focus on building companies, managing employees, stocking locations, and expanding across New York City.

**Target Audience:** Useful for players who want to experiment with business layouts, test logistics networks, accelerate progression, reduce repetitive management tasks, or explore New York City without normal financial and time constraints.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses direct character movement through an open city. The player can walk, drive vehicles, use public transportation, and travel by taxi. Use movement-related trainer options only after the character has loaded into the world, and avoid activating them during transitions or while entering buildings.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory is central to purchasing and transporting products, equipment, furniture, and business supplies. The trainer can reduce carrying limitations and adjust stock quantities, making it easier to furnish locations, restock stores, and test warehouse distribution. Activate inventory options before picking up or transferring items, then disable them before saving if the game behaves unexpectedly.

### World & Environment

The game world is a functional New York business environment containing apartments, stores, offices, warehouses, suppliers, dealerships, workplaces, and properties available for rent or purchase. Players manage personal needs while building businesses, hiring employees, arranging deliveries, and expanding into larger operations. Use money, time, and vehicle options to explore the city and test business infrastructure efficiently.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions is not a combat-focused game and has no conventional fighting, weapons, or enemy encounter system. Progression is driven by entrepreneurship, customer satisfaction, staffing, inventory, transportation, real estate, and logistics rather than combat.
